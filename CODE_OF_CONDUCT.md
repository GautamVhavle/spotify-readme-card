# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent to people. This is a small project about music cards; nothing here is worth
being unpleasant over.

## Expected behaviour

- Assume good faith. Most disagreements are misunderstandings.
- Give feedback on the code, not the person who wrote it.
- Accept that maintainers may decline a change to keep the project small.
- Respect that everyone here is doing this in their spare time.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, intimidation, or discrimination of any kind, including on the basis of
  gender, gender identity, sexual orientation, disability, appearance, body size, race,
  ethnicity, age, religion, or nationality.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention.
- Personal or political attacks, insults, and deliberate provocation.
- Publishing anyone's private information without explicit permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, issues, or reviews.

## Scope

This applies in every project space — issues, pull requests, discussions, commit messages
and code review — and whenever someone is representing the project in public.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainer through
[GitHub](https://github.com/GautamVhavle). Reports are handled privately and in
confidence, and the reporter's identity is not shared.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may take any action deemed appropriate, including editing or removing
content, closing issues or pull requests, and blocking accounts. Responses are
proportionate: a private word first where that is enough, escalating only when it is not.
